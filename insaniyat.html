Story of Insaniyat

“My grandfather once told me:
Never let anyone leave your home empty-handed if they come seeking help.”

Not everyone comes asking for money.
Some need care.

Helping doesn’t always require wealth.
It requires intent, empathy, and the courage to care.

Let’s choose Insaniyat — being human, always.
